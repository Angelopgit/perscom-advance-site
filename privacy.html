1. Overview

PERSCOM Advance ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard information in connection with our website (perscomadvance.com) and our self-hosted Software product.

Because PERSCOM Advance is a self-hosted product, there is a critical distinction between two types of data:

  • Your Unit Data — all data you enter into the Software (personnel records, operations, evaluations, documents, etc.) is stored entirely on your own server. We have no access to this data whatsoever.
  • Account & License Data — information you provide to us directly when subscribing or contacting us, and minimal technical data sent during license validation.

This Privacy Policy applies only to Account & License Data and to visitors of our website.

3. How We Use Information

We use the information we collect solely to:

  • Deliver and manage your license — issuing license keys, validating active subscriptions, managing renewals
  • Process payments — via Stripe, our third-party payment processor
  • Provide customer support — responding to inquiries and troubleshooting license issues
  • Send transactional communications — license delivery, expiry warnings, billing receipts
  • Detect and prevent fraud or abuse — identifying unauthorized use or circumvention of license controls
  • Improve our product and website — understanding usage patterns to enhance our services
  • Comply with legal obligations — as required by applicable law

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Information Sharing

We share your information only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Stripe — our payment processor, for billing and subscription management. Stripe's privacy policy governs their handling of payment data.
  • Email service providers — to send transactional emails (license delivery, billing). These providers are contractually prohibited from using your data for other purposes.
  • Legal compliance — if required by law, subpoena, or other legal process, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of PERSCOM Advance or others.
  • Business transfer — in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, provided the acquiring party agrees to honor this Privacy Policy.
// 05

5. Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational security measures to protect the information we hold, including:

  • Encrypted HTTPS connections for all data transmission
  • Hashed and secured storage of license keys
  • Access controls limiting who can access license records
  • Regular security reviews of our license server infrastructure

No method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security, but we are committed to protecting your information using industry-standard practices.

// 06

6. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as your subscription is active and for a reasonable period thereafter to:

  • Resolve disputes or handle reactivations
  • Comply with legal obligations (e.g., tax and billing records for up to 7 years)
  • Prevent fraud and abuse

Validation logs are retained for up to 90 days, then automatically purged. Upon request, we will delete your personal data subject to legal retention requirements.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Correction — request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Deletion — request deletion of your personal data (subject to legal retention requirements)
  • Portability — request your data in a structured, machine-readable format
  • Objection — object to certain processing of your data

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@perscomadvance.com. We will respond within 30 days.

If you are located in the European Economic Area, you have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.
